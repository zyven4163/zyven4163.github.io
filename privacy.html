Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Zyven Chrome extension handles information when you use the extension.

Information We Access

The extension uses TikTok Login Kit to allow users to securely sign in with their TikTok account. We may access basic TikTok account information permitted by the user.info.basic scope.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through TikTok is used only to provide the functionality of the Zyven extension.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the extension.

Third-Party Services

The extension uses TikTok services. TikTok's own privacy policy also applies to your use of TikTok.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the extension developer.
